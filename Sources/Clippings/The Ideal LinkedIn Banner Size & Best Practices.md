Your LinkedIn banner is a key component of your LinkedIn profile that should not be overlooked. A well-designed background with the right LinkedIn banner size can help you make a strong impression that encourages visitors to engage with your profile.

Your banner, also referred to as a LinkedIn background photo or LinkedIn cover photo, should represent your professional skills and unique personality. For the best results, you need to use a high-quality banner image that is sized correctly.

This guide will cover the ideal LinkedIn banner size and best practices to follow so that you can get more visibility and stand out from the competition.

## LinkedIn Banner Dimensions

For personal LinkedIn profiles, the best LinkedIn banner size is **1584 x 396 pixels**. This size will ensure that your LinkedIn background banner is optimized for desktop and mobile devices.

Additionally, your LinkedIn cover photo should be a **JPG or PNG**  file that is under **8MB** in size.

![Image11](https://sp-ao.shortpixel.ai/client/to_webp,q_glossy,ret_img/https://blog.snappa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image11.jpg)

**  
IMPORTANT NOTE**: For LinkedIn company pages, the banner dimensions are slightly different than personal LinkedIn profile banners. If you want to make a custom cover photo for your LinkedIn company page, your banner size should be 1128 x 191 pixels.

## LinkedIn Banners on Desktop vs. Mobile

Similar to [Facebook cover photos](https://snappa.com/blog/facebook-cover-photo-size/), your custom LinkedIn banner will appear different on desktop and mobile devices, even if you use the correct LinkedIn background banner size.

On desktop devices, your [LinkedIn profile picture](https://snappa.com/templates/profile-picture) will cover a small portion of the bottom-left corner of your LinkedIn banner.

![Image10](https://sp-ao.shortpixel.ai/client/to_webp,q_glossy,ret_img,w_453,h_178/https://blog.snappa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image10.jpg)

On mobile devices, your LinkedIn profile picture will cover a larger portion of the bottom-left corner of your LinkedIn banner.

![Image7](https://sp-ao.shortpixel.ai/client/to_webp,q_glossy,ret_img,w_452,h_264/https://blog.snappa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image7.jpg)

With this in mind, it’s important to use the ideal LinkedIn banner size of **1584 x 396 pixels**, and to position your main design elements in the middle or on the right side of your LinkedIn background photo.

By adding text, images, and graphics to the middle or right side of your background photo, you can ensure that your LinkedIn banner will display perfectly on all devices, and that your LinkedIn profile picture will not cover any important information or visuals.

## What Should My LinkedIn Banner Be?

There are a few LinkedIn banner best practices to follow to maximize effectiveness and increase your LinkedIn profile visibility:

- **Showcase Your Value:** Add design elements to your banner that highlight your value and what you bring to the table. This can be something as simple as a tagline that describes your key skills, or a professional image that showcases your experience.
- **Keep it Relevant:** Make sure that your banner reflects the career or industry that you’re in. When people view your LinkedIn profile, they should be able to determine your role or expertise at a quick glance.
- **Use High-Quality Images & Graphics:** Using [high-quality images](https://snappa.com/blog/free-stock-photos/) will make your banner appear more professional and increase your chances of success. Blurry images or an incorrectly-sized banner may turn away potential opportunities. To avoid any issues, try using Snappa’s [pre-sized LinkedIn templates](https://snappa.com/templates/linkedin-banner).
- **Avoid Clutter:** Keep your banner clean and simple. If you have too much going on in your banner design, viewers may get overwhelmed and click away from your profile.
- **Stay Up-to-Date:** Regularly update your banner design with new skills, services, and information. As your experience grows, your LinkedIn cover photo should reflect that.

## How to Make a Strawberry Margarita

Making a frozen strawberry margarita is very easy, as long as you have a blender or another type of [margarita machine](https://www.allrecipes.com/best-margarita-machines-8637778). Simply pulse the ice in your blender until it's crushed. Then add the rest of the ingredients and blend until smooth. Serve in a margarita glass (with or without a salted rim).

## Ingredients

Original recipe (1X) yields 4 servings

- 2 cups ice cubes, or as needed
- 6 fluid ounces tequila
- 2 fluid ounces triple sec
- 8 ounces frozen sliced strawberries in syrup
- 4 fluid ounces frozen limeade concentrate

## Directions

1. Gather ingredients and fill a blender with ice cubes. Pulse until ice is crushed.
	![Overhead of a margarita assembly area with glasses, salt, and limes off to the side. ](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/tziFPAJn8qaTj-WI9uQCMYbCJ28=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/24494-ultimate-frozen-strawberry-margarita-step1_1912-ca49ab271eb5457ba198f770385f9576.jpg)
	Dotdash Meredith Food Studios
2. Pour in tequila and triple sec, then add strawberries and limeade concentrate.
	![Overhead of a blender with margarita ingredients inside with measuring tools off to the side. ](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/1tOcJHWqA1FyBJI1rsfOqF5TrOc=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/24494-ultimate-frozen-strawberry-margarita-step2_1916-3f2bb22694c041d3bb048a64a53f94c0.jpg)
	Dotdash Meredith Food Studios
3. Blend until smooth, about 30 seconds. Pour into four margarita glasses and serve immediately.
	![Overhead of a blender with a fully blended margarita mix inside. ](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/5ETs8HFRzjsvc4WAdmyLCgw691g=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/24494-ultimate-frozen-strawberry-margarita-step3_1917-424eaa7459514d959da72186e4415f60.jpg)

4. Enjoy!

## ## Ingredients

Original recipe (1X) yields 12 servings

- 1 cup boiling water
- 2 tablespoons instant espresso coffee powder, divided
- 6 tablespoons coffee liqueur, such as Mr. Black, divided
- 2 (8-ounce packages) mascarpone cheese, softened
- 2(8-ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 (7-ounce) package crisp Italian ladyfingers (savoiardi), divided
- 1 cup sliced almonds, toasted
- 2 tablespoons white sugar
- 1/3 cup butter, melted
- 2 cups whipping cream
- unsweetened cocoa powder for dusting, as needed
- 12 chocolate covered espresso beans

## Directions

1. Gather all ingredients.  
	![Ingredients for a no-bake tiramisu cheesecake displayed on a surface, including mascarpone, ladyfingers, and various toppings](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/kDqzaQM2BVPEZdXZbF_Y17ys7-U=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/11748557-No-Bake-Tiramisu-Cheesecake-ddmfs-step01-8048b619767643bd8f68fff6054ddb21.jpg)
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
2. Pour boiling water into a small bowl and stir in 1 tablespoon instant espresso coffee powder until dissolved. Set aside and allow to cool to room temperature, 30 minutes. When cool, add 2 tablespoons coffee liqueur. You will use this mixture to soak ladyfingers for the filling.  
	![Hands whisking a brown mixture in a bowl, with liquid being poured from a small glass container](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/r4w-8bRxC7wEh194jDlbizPnL84=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/11748557-No-Bake-Tiramisu-Cheesecake-ddmfs-step02-f2a266eaa0de458dbf44f4dd45b52200.jpg)
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
3. Meanwhile, for the filling beat mascarpone and cream cheese in a large bowl with an electric mixer on medium until smooth. Beat in powdered sugar, remaining 1 tablespoon instant espresso coffee powder, and salt until well combined. Beat in vanilla and remaining 4 tablespoons coffee liqueur. Cover bowl, and chill in the refrigerator for 30 minutes.  
	![A hand is pouring liquid into a stand mixer with a mixing bowl containing ingredients](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/7xV2alpmfMzSUYU4pmG1pWAAbB8=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/11748557-No-Bake-Tiramisu-Cheesecake-ddmfs-step03-ccbbf59389b6473689294560d0a320a0.jpg)
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
4. For crust, combine 12 ladyfingers, almonds, and white sugar in a food processor. Pulse to fine crumbs. Remove mixture from food processor. Stir in butter until mixture is moistened.  
	![Person mixing melted butter into crushed biscuits in a glass bowl using a plastic spatula](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/4O7mqCUwsxqiAUu6UWgviGUyGBY=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/11748557-No-Bake-Tiramisu-Cheesecake-ddmfs-step04-25064-f549bdaa76d74bdd94f24c4ed84ce11d.jpg)
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
5. Pat crumb mixture evenly into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan. Place pan in the freezer until set, 15 minutes.
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
6. Beat cream in a large bowl with an electric mixer until it forms stiff peaks.  
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
7. Stir one fourth of the whipped cream into the filling mixture to lighten. Fold remaining whipped cream into the filling mixture until combined.  
	![Mixing bowl with creamy no-bake cheesecake filling being folded with a spatula](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/hVa2xVCiCy7mvk0X6B4W3LOg4Aw=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/11748557-No-Bake-Tiramisu-Cheesecake-ddmfs-step07-5d17a8da921d4236a4a60399083a0ed6.jpg)
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
8. Spread half of the filling over the crust.
	![Prepared cheesecake mixture in a round pan next to remaining mixture in a mixing bowl](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/WcDlcO6srHJIPR5wrQfglmHZdEk=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/11748557-No-Bake-Tiramisu-Cheesecake-ddmfs-step08-25074-0ca5f5d13bc94ac7b8c7c067fe3a23ed.jpg)
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
9. Dip remaining 12 ladyfingers, one at a time, into coffee mixture to coat, about 1 second. They will soften quickly and break easily. Place atop the filling, breaking to fit as needed. Spread remaining filling evenly over the ladyfingers.  
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
10. Cover and chill in the refrigerator 6 hours to overnight.  
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
11. Dust cocoa powder generously over top of the cheesecake. Loosen sides of pan with a thin spatula. Remove sides of pan.  
	![Top view of a tiramisu cheesecake covered with cocoa powder, on a light surface](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/aDunuAHXs66RR-UFM1A-U-RO7tY=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/11748557-No-Bake-Tiramisu-Cheesecake-ddmfs-step11-145d48e6ad1042e9896b0a3160c50b8c.jpg)
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
12. Garnish each slice with a chocolate covered espresso bean.  
	![Top view of a tiramisu cheesecake garnished with coffee beans in a circular arrangement](https://www.allrecipes.com/thmb/99umL9qC7HbienDk_UPhORMAOrM=/1500x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/11748557-No-Bake-Tiramisu-Cheesecake-ddmfs-step12-697ea69e3d64498eb7a58a054e4d149d.jpg)
	Photographer: Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Shannon Goforth / Prop Styling: Breanna Ghazali
